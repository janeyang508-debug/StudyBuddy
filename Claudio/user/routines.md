- Wake up, make coffee
- Music mood: gentle start, nothing too loud
- Preferred: soft acoustic, light jazz, or calm mandopop

## Work / Study Block
- Need focus music, low distraction
- Preferred: instrumental, lo-fi, no lyrics or familiar lyrics only

## Evening (18:00 – 21:00)
- Unwinding after work
- Preferred: chill, warm, slower tempo

## Weekend
- More adventurous, try new things
- Open to recommendations outside my usual taste
